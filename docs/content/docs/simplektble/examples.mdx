---
title: Examples
description: Runnable Bluetooth client and GATT hosting examples for SimpleKtBLE.
---

The [examples](https://github.com/simpleble/simpleble/tree/main/examples/simplektble)
live alongside the other SimpleBLE binding examples:

- **ListAdapters**: Check whether Bluetooth is enabled and print each adapter.
- **ListBackends**: Enumerate backends and list the adapters provided by each one.
- **Scan**: Scan for five seconds, printing peripherals with their signal strength as they are found and updated, then whether each is connectable and its manufacturer data.
- **Connect**: Connect to a peripheral and list its services, characteristics with their capabilities, and descriptors.
- **Read**: Connect to a peripheral and print a GATT characteristic in hex.
- **Write**: Connect to a peripheral and write text to a GATT characteristic.
- **Notify**: Connect to a peripheral and print the notifications of a characteristic for five seconds.
- **Peripheral**: Host a GATT service that echoes writes to subscribed clients and prints client and subscription events.
- **Dongl**: Scan with a Dongl adapter, then connect and answer pairing requests.

The examples are a Gradle project for the `jvm` target that builds SimpleKtBLE
from the checkout. Run one from the repository root with its task:

```sh
utils/gradle/gradlew -p examples/simplektble runScan
```

Each example has a task named after it, from `runListAdapters` to `runDongl`.
The examples ask for their choices on the console, and `runPeripheral` runs
until you press Ctrl+C.

For binding development, see the
[development guide](https://github.com/simpleble/simpleble/blob/main/docs/extras/simplektble-development.md).
For events, lifetime, and handlers, see [Usage](./usage).
